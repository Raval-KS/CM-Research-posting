Methodological Addendum

While this instrument collects baseline data on student well-being, its primary function is methodological validation. This study evaluates the statistical reliability of unsupervised undergraduate research by analyzing completion latencies and embedded psychometric engagement indices. The purpose is to quantify the prevalence of Insufficient Effort Responding (IER) within standard medical student convenience samples.

Your data, including your submission timestamps and response pattern validation scores, have been logged completely anonymously. This aggregate data will be utilized to formulate evidence-based guidelines for improving the rigor of future questionnaire-based research at our institution.
